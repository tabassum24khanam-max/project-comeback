01 — About SHE

A Saudi-owned house of expertise, built over five decades.

Founded in 1971 and headquartered in Riyadh, Saudi House of Expertise (SHE) is a single-source procurement and supply partner to businesses, projects and institutions across the Kingdom.

We source and supply high-quality materials and equipment from reputed local and international manufacturers, with a proven track record of prompt, efficient, nationwide service. We manage the supply chain from start to end — so our clients deal with one accountable partner, not a dozen vendors.

Our Journey

Five decades of range and reliability

1971

A Saudi trading house is founded

SHE opens in Riyadh as a Saudi-owned trading and supply establishment, built on service and long-term relationships.

Heritage

The House of Beverage Solutions

For a chapter of its journey, SHE equipped hotels, restaurants and cafés end-to-end — espresso machines, specialty coffee, syrups and teas — proving it could outfit entire operations.

Today

Diversified trading & procurement

SHE supplies twelve product lines — from construction and healthcare to safety, electrical and industrial — as a single-source partner across the Kingdom.

Vision 2030

Supplying the Kingdom's giga-projects

Today SHE supports many of Saudi Arabia's landmark developments — from KAFD and Qiddiya to NEOM, ROSHN and the Riyadh Metro — as part of the nation's transformation.
